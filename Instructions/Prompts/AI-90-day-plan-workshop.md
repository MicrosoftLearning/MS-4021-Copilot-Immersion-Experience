# Build Your 90-day AI Plan

Define a clear **12-month AI aspiration** and turn one initiative into a focused **90-day plan**.

Copilot acts as a **strategic thinking partner**. You bring the problem. Copilot helps you shape the aspiration, choose a focus, and strengthen the plan.

You will:

1. Name the problem you want to work on
2. Define your AI aspiration (12 months)
3. Identify key AI initiatives
4. Prioritize one 90-day focus
5. Design your 90-day plan
6. Review your draft plan *(extra, if you have time)*
7. Create a 1-minute executive pitch *(extra, if you have time)*

> **TIMING:** Steps 1–5 are the core of this exercise and take about 35 minutes: about 5 minutes each for Steps 1–4, then about 15 minutes for Step 5. Your 90-day plan is complete at the end of Step 5. Steps 6 and 7 are extras for anyone with time left, about 5 minutes each.

> **NOTE:** Sample prompts are provided to help you get started. Feel free to personalize them for your role, organization, and priorities.
>
> You can also use Copilot as an interactive coach on any step by typing that step's helper phrase in chat (for example, **"Help me brainstorm a problem"** or **"Help me draft the 90-day plan"**).

## Step 1: Name the Problem

**Suggested time:** About 5 minutes

Write the business problem you want this plan to address.

Focus on a **business-level** problem in the area you lead. Not a company-wide transformation, and not individual productivity such as summarizing your email or getting through your own task list faster.

Include:

- What you lead
- What is not moving
- Why it matters

Rough is fine. If you are not sure where to look, start from whichever value area is slowest or leaking value.

| Value area | Example, not your answer |
| --- | --- |
| Enrich employee experiences | Field and frontline teams cannot get the current procedure, or an expert, while the job is in progress. First-time resolution stays low, and the same work gets redone. |
| Reinvent customer engagement | Customer requests sit until someone manually routes them. Response time and the answer depend on who happens to be free. |
| Reshape business processes | Replenishment or production decisions are made from yesterday's report. Stockouts, downtime, or excess show up before anyone can act. |
| Bend the curve on innovation | New designs wait on a small group of specialists to draft and revise them. The launch cycle is longer than the demand shift you are trying to meet. |

Write your own problem. Do not copy the example.

> **Coach option:** Want a thinking partner to brainstorm or sharpen your problem? Type **`Help me brainstorm a problem`** in Copilot Chat.

You will paste this problem into the next prompt.

## Step 2: Define Your AI Aspiration (12-month)

**Suggested time:** About 5 minutes

Start from the problem you named. Focus on a high-impact business outcome, not a tool rollout and not individual productivity.

**Sample Prompt:**

```text
For my [insert business unit or function], I want to address this business problem:

[paste your problem]

Identify how AI could materially drive value on this problem over the next 12 months.

Focus on business-level outcomes, not individual productivity.

Capture:
- Title
- Business problem or opportunity
- Why it matters

Then define:
- 1–2 measurable success metrics
- A clear AI aspiration
```

> **Capture your response** in Word, OneNote, or Copilot Pages. You will use these notes in the next steps.

### Define What Success Looks Like

Describe the measurable outcomes or KPIs that would indicate success if your AI aspiration were realized.

**Sample Prompt:**

```text
If we successfully achieved this AI aspiration, what measurable outcomes or changes would indicate success? Provide examples of metrics or observable improvements.
```

> **Capture your response** for use in the next steps.
>
> **Coach option:** Want help turning your problem into a measurable 12-month outcome? Type **`Help me define my 12-month aspiration`** in Copilot Chat.

## Step 3: Identify Your AI Initiatives

**Suggested time:** About 5 minutes

Now break this aspiration into smaller, actionable initiatives.

**Sample Prompt:**

```text
Based on this AI aspiration:

[paste your aspiration]

Suggest 2–3 practical AI initiatives that could help achieve this outcome.
```

Write down:

- **One existing initiative contributing to this aspiration**
- **One new opportunity that could accelerate progress toward it**

> Keep this short. Just a name and a one-sentence description for each.
>
> **Coach option:** Need help shaping an existing and a new initiative? Type **`Help me identify initiatives`** in Copilot Chat.

## Step 4: Prioritize Your Focus

**Suggested time:** About 5 minutes

Select the initiative most likely to deliver measurable value in the next 90 days.

**Sample Prompt:**

```text
Given my AI aspiration and these initiatives:

[paste your aspiration and initiatives]

What is the biggest gap or risk that could prevent success?

Which initiative would create the most measurable value in the next 90 days, and why?
```

You should now have:

- One clear **AI aspiration (12-month outcome)**
- Two initiatives (**current + new**)
- One **prioritized initiative** to take forward

> **Coach option:** Want to pressure-test the gap and narrow your 90-day scope? Type **`Help me prioritize focus`** in Copilot Chat.

## Step 5: Design Your 90-day Plan

**Suggested time:** About 15 minutes. This step completes your 90-day plan.

You are not solving the full aspiration yet. You are defining a focused entry point that moves it forward.

Outline your plan using the template below. Use it as a **thinking framework**. Document your responses in Word, OneNote, Copilot Pages, or another tool of your choice.

Focus on **outcomes, impact, and measurability**, not tools or implementation details.

![AI 90-day plan Template](../Prompts/Media/lighthouse-template.png)

### 1. Strategic Intent — *Why this, why now?*

**Consider:**

- What business problem or opportunity are you addressing in the next 90 days?
- Why is now the right moment to act?

### 2. Value Hypothesis — *Where can AI move the needle?*

Describe how AI will create value using a simple cause-and-effect statement.

**Format:**

> *If we do X, then we will achieve Y, because Z.*

### 3. Proof of Value — *How will we validate success?*

**Success metric:**

- What is the **one measurable outcome or KPI** that defines success for your plan?

**Scope and guardrails:**

- What is the **smallest viable scope** that still demonstrates value?
- What is explicitly **out of scope**?

### 4. Actions — *Who will lead, and what comes first?*

**Accountable owner:**

- Who will own and champion the plan internally?

**First actions:**

- What **1–2 concrete steps** will you take to get this started?

### 5. Storytelling — *How does this scale?*

If this plan succeeds:

- How could it be replicated across teams, regions, or business units?
- What elements make it reusable or repeatable?
- Which Frontier pattern does it move you toward: **human with assistant**, **human-agent teams**, or **human-led, agent-operated**? Where does human judgment stay?

## Step 6: Review Your Draft with Copilot

**Extra step, if you have time:** About 5 minutes. Your 90-day plan is already complete; this step strengthens it for executive stakeholders.

Once you have drafted your plan, use Copilot to review and strengthen it.

**Sample Prompt:**

```text
Here is my draft of a 90-day plan for an AI initiative.

Please review it and suggest how I can make it more clear, compelling, and measurable for executive stakeholders. Focus your feedback on clarity, focus, and business impact.

Based on what you see in the problem and plan I shared, what would you recommend I improve or clarify?

[paste your draft]
```

> **NOTE:**
> To share your draft with Copilot, take a screenshot of your completed plan and paste it into Copilot:
>
> - **Windows:** Press **Windows + Shift + S** to capture your plan
> - **Mac:** Press **Command + Shift + 4** to capture your plan
>
> You can also paste your draft text directly into Copilot instead of uploading a screenshot, or type **`Review my draft plan`** to run a focused executive check.

## Step 7: Create a 1-Minute Executive Pitch

**Extra step, if you have time:** About 5 minutes.

Use Copilot to help you turn your 90-day plan into a **concise, 1-minute pitch**.

**Sample Prompt:**

```text
Based on my updated 90-day plan, help me create a concise 1-minute pitch for my key stakeholder:
[describe your stakeholder, e.g., "Chief Operating Officer responsible for digital transformation"].

The pitch should clearly explain the business problem, the proposed approach, the expected impact, how success will be measured, and why this matters.

Please make the language clear, compelling, and suitable for an executive audience.

Here is my draft:
[paste your revised 90-day plan or summary here]
```

> **Coach option:** Want to tailor the pitch to a specific stakeholder and test likely objections? Type **`Help me build the 1-minute pitch`** in Copilot Chat.

## Outcome of This Exercise

By the end of Step 5, you should have:

- One clear **AI aspiration (12-month outcome)**
- Two initiatives (**current + new**)
- One **prioritized initiative**
- A **90-day plan**

If you completed the extra steps, you also have:

- A **strengthened 90-day plan**, reviewed with Copilot
- A **1-minute executive pitch**
